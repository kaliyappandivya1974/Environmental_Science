!INTERPRETATION

Read model scores carefully

Risk scores are Random Forest model scores, not calibrated probabilities. The water anomaly detector is unsupervised and its output is not an official water-quality determination.

All models were trained and evaluated on synthetic records. Synthetic data may produce unrealistically optimistic results.
